About

Designing with memory and responsibility.

I am a May 2026 Bachelor of Architecture graduate from the New Jersey Institute of Technology, seeking a full-time role in architecture or construction.

My work moves across the full design pipeline—from computational modeling to production-ready construction documentation—with a focus on adaptive reuse, urban regeneration, and socially responsive design.

I combine technical rigor with site history, environmental analysis, and narrative to create architecture that is functional, meaningful, and open to change.

02 / ARCH 495 · Ecological Civic Space

Liberty Rail Gardens

Liberty State Park, New Jersey
Landscape + Civic Pavilion
UDIA, SDA + ASE Analysis
0  1  3  5
Rooted in the ecological and maritime history of Liberty State Park and the neighboring Communipaw settlement, this project reintroduces indigenous plant and aquatic species through programmed pavilions, aquaponic farms, and exhibition spaces. The building's undulating façade traces water and human movement, while five funnel elements punctuate the roof — metaphorical stones breaking a river's current.
